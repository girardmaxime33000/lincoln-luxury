Three ways to shape your day

Every wine tour is built to order, but here are three examples of the days we compose, step by step.

  • Left Bank — MédocMargaux, Pauillac, Saint-Estèphe, along the châteaux route.

Three possible directions from Bordeaux, never combined on the same day: each circuit keeps the time each stop needs.

Left Bank

Médoc, châteaux & grand crus

A day along the châteaux route, across three of the Médoc’s best-known appellations.

Right Bank

Saint-Émilion & Pomerol

The Libournais and its great terroirs, at a tighter pace, less than an hour from the city.

  4. 2:00 PMSaint-Émilion — estate & tasting

Heading south

Pessac-Léognan & Sauternes

From the Graves at Bordeaux’s doorstep to the sweet wines of the Sauternais, in the morning mist.

  3. 12:00 PMLunch en route to the Sauternais
  4. 2:00 PMSauternes — visit & tasting

≈ 100 km · 2 estates · full day

08Questions

What we’re asked

If your question isn’t listed, write to us: we answer ourselves.

Where does a wine tour start and end?

Outside your hotel, in Bordeaux or nearby, and it ends there in the evening. We stay with you from morning to evening, for the whole length of your stay.

How long does a stay last?

Usually three to six days, with days organised in advance. Single days or half-days are also possible, depending on our availability.

Which car takes to the road?

A Mercedes S-Class. We’re the only chauffeur service in Bordeaux to offer it for this kind of service, where the local market runs on the E-Class.

Do you organise the estate visits?

Yes, we prepare the itinerary with you in advance: the estates, the order of the stops, travel times and tasting times. You have nothing to coordinate on site.

Do you do anything besides wine tours?

Yes. Airport and train station transfers, half-day or full-day chauffeur service, business travel and evenings out.

What are your rates?

Every request gets a tailored proposal, based on the length, the itinerary and the number of passengers. We send it to you before any booking.

Can we count on your discretion?

It’s the foundation of the job. No client name is ever mentioned, no photo is ever published, no route detail is ever shared.

Do you work with an international clientele?

A large share of our passengers come from abroad, often as part of organised stays. Write to us in whichever language suits you best.
